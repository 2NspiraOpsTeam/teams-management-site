## Accessibility findings and remedies

Reproduced on the built baseline:

1. Resident fine print: contrast 4.49:1 on the home card and 4.45:1 on the account card, below 4.5:1 for small text. Darkened the shared fine-print color from `#657064` to `#5f695d`.
2. Admin metric cards: an anchor interrupted valid definition-list grouping. Changed the outer collection to `ul/li` and gave each linked metric its own `dl/dt/dd`; links, labels, counts and visual styles are preserved.
3. Renewal review/follow-up links: 16px-high hit targets with insufficient surrounding space at different widths. Both now have a 24px minimum height and centered inline-flex content.

Baseline expanded scan: 18 authenticated routes at 1280px and 390px (36 scans). The findings above appeared on resident home/account, Admin home and Rent Roll. No document-wide horizontal overflow was detected in these scans. This supplements, rather than supersedes, the earlier 48-public-page scan.

Additional six-screen review covered public home, rental inquiry, News, shared access, synthetic resident home and synthetic Admin home. First 22 sequential Tab stops per screen had visible nonzero focus targets and focus indicators. At 640px and 320px, no document-wide horizontal overflow was detected. Public mobile menu Escape returned focus to its opener. Screenshots were visually inspected for resident/Admin home and the rental form at 320px. This is a bounded keyboard/visual engineering review, not a full manual assistive-technology audit.

## Isolated load results

Mixed authenticated read routes: session, leases, contacts, household, maintenance, and Admin operations overview. Six phases completed 29,433 requests in 185.43 seconds with zero unexpected failures and zero database rows written according to local D1 metering.

| Phase | Concurrency | Duration | Requests | Requests/sec | p95 latency |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Step 1 | 1 | 15.00 s | 4,036 | 269.0 | 5.4 ms |
| Step 2 | 8 | 15.02 s | 6,435 | 428.5 | 25.7 ms |
| Step 3 | 24 | 15.04 s | 6,989 | 464.6 | 70.1 ms |
| Step 4 | 48 | 15.08 s | 7,437 | 493.3 | 135.1 ms |
| Paced endurance | 8 | 120.20 s | 4,492 | 37.4 | 24.4 ms |
| Post-load recovery | 1 | 5.09 s | 44 | 8.6 | 17.7 ms |

The load generator was restricted to loopback, used 8-second request timeouts and stopped on more than ten failures. No failure threshold was reached. The endurance phase included 200ms pacing per virtual worker and recovery included 100ms pacing, so their throughput is deliberately capped. Measurements include local runtime/database overhead on one workstation; browser spot checks and source inspection also occurred during the run. This was not a dedicated benchmark host.

**No hosted-capacity claim:** 48 concurrent in-flight requests is not 48 users or a production traffic limit. These are local SQLite-backed D1 readings, not Cloudflare regional D1/network/quota results. This short endurance test is not a multi-hour soak, failure-injection drill or saturation/breakpoint determination. Concurrent write/retry correctness was exercised separately; the high-throughput test was read-only. Hosted peak capacity, production mail under load and real-world availability remain unverified.
