# Teams Management staff quick guide

## Start with a building

Open **Properties**, choose a building, then use **Overview, Apartments, Tenants, Leases, Rent Roll, Maintenance, or Superintendent**. Select an apartment to see its current occupants and history. Tenant names open the same tenant record everywhere. **Back to previous workspace** returns to your prior list; directory filters are retained in that browser tab.

A missing tenant assignment means **occupancy needs review**, not that an apartment is vacant. Only explicitly confirmed vacancies are described as vacant.

## Find and update contacts

Under **Tenants**, switch between **Tenant Directory**, **Phone List**, and **Import / Upload**. Open a tenant's name, choose **Edit record**, make verified changes, and **Save**. **Cancel** discards a draft after confirmation. A save confirmation appears after the server accepts the change.

Keep an imported full name intact unless you know the correct first and last names. Confirmed name components update the shared display name. Add phone and email contacts with their appropriate labels; retain alternate and CC contacts. Choose a primary only when confirmed. Set the preferred contact method separately.

Contact emails are not login identities. Contact edits neither activate tenant access nor send invitations. Invitations are on hold.

## Use the Phone List

The Phone List defaults to **Current Occupants**. Choose a property, search a name/phone/email/address/apartment, or find missing phone/email information. **Historical Occupants** and **Current & Historical Occupants** are explicit alternatives. Each occupant has a separate row, with all recorded contacts.

- **Print Current Building** prints all records matching that building's applied filters, including historical occupants only when selected.
- **Print Entire Portfolio** prints current occupants across all properties, regardless of the on-screen search or historical filter.
- The browser opens its normal print dialog. The report uses US Letter landscape, building headings, repeating column headers, generation time in Eastern time, and no Admin controls. Page-number support depends on the browser.
- **Export Excel** and **Export CSV** use the applied filters, across all matching pages.

Prints and downloads contain private contact information. Keep them with authorized staff; do not distribute them publicly.

## Enter and renew leases

Open an apartment or tenant, then **Add lease**. Select an existing property/apartment, add the correct leaseholders, and enter verified dates, monthly rent, status, and renewal information. Security deposit is optional. Current validation requires both dates, monthly rent, and at least one leaseholder. Never enter guessed dates or $0 to stand in for missing rent.

Open an agreement and choose **Update lease** to correct it. Use **Renew / new agreement** for a successor; the previous agreement and its history remain available. Review the status of the previous agreement when a renewal takes effect. The Rent Roll flags overlapping current agreements for review.

Signed PDF uploads remain disabled until secure private document storage is approved and configured. Lease records remain usable without uploads. Saving a lease does not email or publish it to tenants.

## Use the Rent Roll

Open **Rent Roll** for the portfolio or a building's **Rent Roll** section. There is one row per apartment. The report selects a current agreement first, then a future agreement, then the latest historical agreement; the lease-status column identifies what is displayed. Open the apartment for its complete lease history.

Current occupants and leaseholders are shown separately because they need not be identical. Missing amounts are **Not supplied**, never estimated. **No current lease** can include apartments with only upcoming or historical agreements. Refresh after another staff member makes a change.

## Superintendents and maintenance

Under **Superintendents**, add verified contact information and select one or more assigned properties. Assignments appear under those same properties. Tenant contact sharing is separate and off unless explicitly approved. No contacts are invented.

Open **Maintenance** or a property's Maintenance section to review requests, status, history, and staff assignments. Internal notes remain private. Saving a status change updates the shared request rather than creating another request.

## Dashboard and exports

Dashboard numbers open their matching lists. Missing-information metrics identify review work, not assumed occupancy or financial information.

Major operational lists include **Export**, with Excel and CSV options. Exports include every matching page up to 1,000 records; narrow the property/search filters if the limit is exceeded. Excel preserves apartment identifiers/ZIP codes as text and keeps recorded amounts numeric. CSV safely escapes special characters and neutralizes formula-like text; Excel is recommended when spreadsheet auto-formatting could change identifiers.

If loading or saving fails, read the message and use Retry. A failed save retains the draft. Do not assume a change succeeded without its confirmation. Imports with uncertain property/unit mappings remain on hold; do not resolve them by guessing or creating duplicate records.
